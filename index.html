About Sophifold

Compact by design.
Ready to reach.

Sophifold combines a tracked mobile base, a two-joint folding structure and a robotic arm. It folds down for a compact profile, then unfolds to raise the arm's working position.

Explore mobile manipulation: drive to a workspace, unfold, and transfer an object between two positions. The interactive demo illustrates that sequence.

01

Fold down

Two folding joints bring the upper structure back over the chassis.

02

Rise to work

Both shells move upright to elevate the robotic arm.

03

Move and manipulate

A tracked base and gripper combine mobility with object handling.
